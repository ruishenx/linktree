#Ask what would change your mind

A research question becomes useful when different answers imply different next actions.

#Replace broad nouns

Instead of asking whether “quality improved,” name the population, behavior, window, and comparison.

#Seek disconfirming evidence

Look for an observation that the favored explanation predicts poorly.

#Keep scope negotiable

Start with a narrow question that can be answered. Expand only after the first answer reveals a justified boundary.
