About Examen Rationis

A slower publication for clearer decisions.

Examen Rationis is a publication about the structures underneath good work: how teams make decisions, how systems learn, and how useful constraints create room for better ideas.

What you will receive

Every other Thursday, one carefully edited field note arrives in your inbox. Each issue offers a practical model, a concrete example, and a question worth carrying into your own work.

Who writes it

I’m Mara Lin, an independent researcher and product strategist. I have spent the last decade helping small teams turn ambiguous problems into durable products. Examen Rationis is where I write down the patterns that keep proving useful.

The publishing rhythm

Examen Rationis is free and published fortnightly. There are no daily alerts, gated archives, or attention tricks. If an idea needs another week, it gets another week.

For questions or collaborations, write to hello@examenrationis.example.
