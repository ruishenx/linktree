Editorial principles

The rules that keep My Philosophy useful, calm, and independent.

My Philosophy is built around four editorial commitments.

Useful before novel

An idea earns space here when a reader can apply it, test it, or use it to see a familiar problem differently.

Specific before sweeping

Concrete mechanisms matter more than big claims. Sources are linked, uncertainty is named, and examples carry the argument.

Calm before constant

Publication frequency is a constraint, not a target. The archive should remain valuable after the week an essay was published.

Independent by design

The reading experience and archive remain available without a social account, proprietary app, or newsletter platform.
